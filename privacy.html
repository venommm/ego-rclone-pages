Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

1. Overview

EGOs Server Backup is a private backup system operated by its owner. It uses rclone and the Google Drive API to transfer backup archives between a privately managed server and the owner's Google Drive account. It is not a public service and is not intended for use by other users.

2. Google Account Data Access

With the account owner's authorization, rclone accesses Google Drive to list files and folders, upload backup archives, and verify uploaded files. The system may access file metadata and contents as permitted by the granted OAuth permissions.

3. Use of Data

Google Drive data is used solely to perform personal backup, synchronization, verification, and recovery operations. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or selling data.

4. Storage and Security

Backup archives are created on the owner's server and encrypted before being uploaded to Google Drive. OAuth credentials and tokens are maintained in the server's local rclone configuration. Access to the server and Google account is controlled by the owner.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Sharing and Third Parties

The system does not independently sell or disclose Google Drive data to third parties. Google Drive is used as the backup storage provider and processes data according to Google's applicable terms and privacy policies. Data may be accessible to anyone with whom the account owner independently shares the relevant files.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Backup files are retained according to the owner's backup and retention configuration. The owner can delete stored backups and revoke the OAuth authorization through the relevant account settings.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the backup system or its data handling practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
